# Privacy

Sonora has no account system, analytics, advertisements or application backend.

Search terms and playback requests are sent directly to YouTube through NewPipeExtractor. Stream requests go to the media hosts returned by that source. With **About → Faster playback starts** enabled (the default), Sonora also resolves up to two likely tracks while a screen is visible and prepares the next queue item during playback. It may contact YouTube, podcast feeds and audio hosts before you press Play, fetching at most 192 KiB of audio per preparation plus metadata/network overhead. Up to four prefixes (768 KiB) are kept only in RAM, with a five-minute reuse limit; audio is never saved to disk. Disable the switch to cancel speculative work and clear the shared prefix cache. Normal playback buffering and artwork requests still work. Preparation never plays audio or creates listening-history entries.

Artwork requests go to the image hosts returned by the source. Those services receive standard network information such as your IP address. Their policies apply to those requests.

Podcast search terms and locale country are sent to Apple’s public podcast directory only when you search. Opening or refreshing a feed contacts its publisher. Playing or preparing an episode fetches its current feed and audio enclosure, including the publisher’s redirect/measurement services. Podcast publishers may include ads in their audio. No Apple or Google account, cookies or paid-feed credentials are used. Podcast subscriptions, cached episode metadata, and listening positions are stored locally; no periodic background subscription polling is performed. Unsubscribe in the podcast screen; this retains cached episodes and progress. Mark unplayed resets an episode’s saved progress.

Favorites, playlists and their track order, canonical track URLs, titles, artwork URLs and listening history stay in a local Room database. Android app backup is disabled. Clear history in Library → History; remove favorites with the heart button. Uninstalling Sonora or clearing its app storage removes the local database. The queue and its last saved position are stored locally; resolved playback URLs remain in memory; Coil may cache thumbnails in app-private storage.

Imported ZIP/CSV files are read locally through the Android document picker. Sonora does not upload the archive or request Google credentials, cookies or account access. Optional missing-title lookup sends canonical video references to YouTube through NewPipeExtractor; disable it in the preview to import offline. Public playlist-link imports also contact YouTube. Artwork may subsequently be fetched when displayed. Files are parsed in memory and are not retained by Sonora. Delete imported playlists from Library → Playlists; favorites are managed separately.

Library backups are created only when you choose Export library and a document destination. They contain metadata, playlist order, favorites and listening timestamps/counts, podcast subscriptions and episode positions in readable JSON, not audio or account credentials. A cloud document provider receives the file only if you select that provider. The restore operation reads and validates the chosen file locally and merges it without contacting YouTube. Displayed artwork and enabled foreground playback preparation can subsequently make network requests. Sonora does not encrypt exported backups.

No storage, microphone, location or account permission is requested. Internet, network-state, foreground media service and wake-lock permissions support streaming and background playback. Android media-session notifications are exempt from the Android 13 notification runtime permission, so Sonora does not request unrelated notification access.

The beta does not collect diagnostic logs remotely. If reporting a bug, avoid including signed stream URLs, private information or an unredacted full device log.
