# Manage playlists

Open **Library → Playlists → New playlist**, enter a name and save. Open a playlist and use the pencil to rename it. Names may contain up to 180 characters.

Song rows in Search, Home and Library expose a three-dot menu: **Add to playlist** opens your local lists and also lets you **Create and add**. The full player has an Add to playlist button. Adding an existing song to the same playlist is harmless; it remains a single entry. **Add to queue** is a separate menu action.

Inside a playlist, hold a drag handle and move it vertically. Near the top/bottom, the list scrolls to reach more entries. The new order is saved when you release the handle. Song menus also provide **Move up** and **Move down** for precise ordering and accessibility. **Remove from playlist** affects only that list; favorites and listening history are kept. Deleting a playlist asks for confirmation and also preserves favorites/history.

Imported playlists are editable. Reimporting merges missing songs, keeping your custom name and existing order. Concurrent imports during a reorder retain newly added songs at the end. None of these actions modifies your YouTube account.

The playback queue is independent of playlist edits: changing a saved playlist does not unexpectedly change the queue already playing. Play all again to load the updated order.

## Shuffle

Use the Shuffle icon beside **Play all** to start a playlist in random order. While listening, **Shuffle up next** in the full player or **Up next** queue shuffles only the pending tracks. It keeps the current track, position, play/pause state and already-played order. The button needs at least two pending tracks. Tap again for another random order; saved playlist order is unchanged. The displayed queue, Next button and restored queue follow the shuffled order.

## Download a playlist

Since 0.8, choose **Download playlist**, confirm the current selection, and manage transfers in **Library → Downloads**. Downloading or removing audio does not change the playlist order or membership. New playlist additions are not automatically downloaded. See [offline listening](DOWNLOADS.md).
